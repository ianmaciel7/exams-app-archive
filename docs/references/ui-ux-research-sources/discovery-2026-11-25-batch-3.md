# Independent UI/UX discovery — batch 3 (2026-11-25)

Scope: general-purpose UI/UX research, independent of any product. Sources discovered via public search-index results (titles and excerpts); no claim of full article reading, media capture, code testing, or measured popularity in 2026. Canonical URLs are retained without tracking parameters. Use source kind and limitations when citing; check access, original article and licence before reuse. No third-party media copied.

## Reusable synthesis

- **Interaction frequency and motion cost:** frequent actions benefit from immediate response and interruption-safe motion; special moments may justify richer animation. Duration recommendations are heuristics, not universal standards. Compare repeated-use and first-use tasks. Respect reduced motion; don't delay actions or hide errors.
- **Spatial continuity:** origin-aware transitions and transforms can communicate the relationship between controls and panels. Preserve real object identity, keyboard focus and scroll independently of visual animation. A sampled CSS easing curve does not implement physical state; use a real spring when interactive retargeting matters.
- **Direct manipulation and accelerators:** let users act on visible objects with incremental feedback, undo and alternative keyboard/explicit actions. Explore scrubbable values, smart snapping, fuzzy command search, keyboard navigation and peek. A palette should accelerate the normal visible routes, not replace them. These are candidates, not universal defaults.
- **Complex professional tools:** research actual tasks, domain and interruption patterns before choosing information density, panel count and shortcuts. Preserve selection, filters, drafts and restoration; separate destructive actions from reversible edits. Test novice and expert paths.
- **Local-first vs malleable software:** local-first concerns ownership, local persistence and collaboration; malleability concerns adapting the tool to the user's needs. These are separate architectural and interaction goals, not visual styles. Validate offline recovery, export, conflicts, customization/undo and access scopes.
- **Information before interaction:** consider whether important information can be shown directly instead of requiring a click or hover. Compare static representation, contextual reveal and direct manipulation. Live previews and transparent transformations can support learning, but test actual comprehension.
- **Evidence:** distinguish conceptual prototypes, portfolio concepts, actual screenshots, recorded interactions, practitioner's hypotheses, formal research, and survey results. Use qualitative observation for discovering problems; quantitative benchmarks for comparing task success, time and errors; surveys measure reported perceptions. No one source proves universal usability or market prevalence. Research papers and books must be read at the relevant section before making specific claims.
- **Progressive enhancement:** essential interaction and semantic content should work before optional effects. Test keyboard, touch, focus, reduced motion, slow devices and unsupported capabilities; verify browser/library versions. For spatial interfaces and game UX, transfer concepts only when task and modality match.

## Sources — new records

- **[Screenrove — product screens](https://screenrove.com/)** — `gallery-real-product`. Capturas de produtos e guias; confirmar versão e origem da captura. Visualizar composição e densidade, não inferir comportamento de screenshots.
- **[Screenrove — Motion](https://screenrove.com/motion)** — `gallery-motion`. Clipes de motion e referências a implementações; verificar licença de cada exemplo. Identificar gatilho, transição, estado final e interrupção.
- **[HashBuilds — Design Patterns](https://www.hashbuilds.com/patterns)** — `community-pattern-library`. Catálogo com demonstrações de padrões. Usar como descoberta de nomenclatura; validar semântica e acessibilidade em fontes primárias.
- **[UI/UX Atlas — Interactive Patterns](https://uiuxatlas.com/patterns/)** — `community-pattern-library`. Exemplos interativos de navegação e command palette. Comparar variantes por custo de interação; demonstração não é teste de usabilidade.
- **[Awesome UX Patterns](https://github.com/bennycode/awesome-ux-patterns)** — `community-catalog`. Indexa aplicativos reais por interação nomeada. Buscar exemplos de scrubbable values, snapping, undo, peek, fuzzy search; conferir no aplicativo.
- **[UI Bible — Desktop Patterns](https://ui.skylanex.com/desktop-patterns)** — `community-guidance`. Orientações sobre produtividade, atalhos e paleta de comandos. Manter navegação visível; paleta funciona como acelerador, não único caminho.
- **[UX Encyclopedia — Desktop Software](https://ux.detroit3d.com/platforms/desktop-software.html)** — `community-guidance`. Diretório sobre ferramentas profissionais, menus, produtividade e atalhos. Distinguir convenções dos aplicativos de requisitos de plataforma.
- **[UX Patterns — Command Palette](https://uxpatterns.dev/patterns/advanced/command-palette)** — `community-pattern-library`. Página específica de command palette, descoberta, comandos e destinos. Testar foco, escape, ranking, estados vazios e execução explícita.
- **[UI Peeps — Game UX/UI resources](https://uipeeps.com/resources)** — `community-catalog`. Diretório de UX de jogos e referências de motion. Explorar feedback, HUD e orientação; não importar padrões de jogos automaticamente para produtividade.
- **[Dribbble — desktop app interfaces](https://dribbble.com/search/desktop-app-interface)** — `gallery-concepts`. Conceitos e portfólios de desktop. Separar propostas de produtos implementados; não confundir visual com usabilidade.
- **[Val Head — UI animation resources](https://valhead.com/ui-animation/)** — `practitioner-guidance`. Curadoria de fundamentos e recursos de animação web. Definir intenção e acessibilidade do movimento antes de escolher efeito.
- **[Emil Kowalski — You Don’t Need Animations](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/you-dont-need-animations)** — `practitioner-guidance`. Discute frequência, percepção e custo do motion. Reduzir ou eliminar animação em ações repetidas; tempos sugeridos são heurísticas.
- **[Emil Kowalski — Great Animations](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/great-animations)** — `practitioner-guidance`. Discute qualidade de motion, desempenho e preferências de movimento. Medir desempenho real e respeitar reduced motion.
- **[Emil Kowalski — Origin-aware animations](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/origin-aware-animations)** — `practitioner-guidance`. Exemplos de origem espacial de transições. Relacionar movimento ao elemento disparador; manter foco e estado sem depender da animação.
- **[Emil Kowalski — CSS Transforms](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/css-transforms)** — `practitioner-guidance`. Transformações CSS usadas em componentes de UI. Testar escala, origem, clipping, legibilidade e fallback.

## Research procedure

Start with a concrete interaction or user problem; retrieve terminology from a pattern catalogue; compare multiple real products and input modes; use practitioner and research sources to formulate hypotheses; verify behavior, licenses and accessibility; record source URLs and dates. Preserve prior links, including superseded ones, with status. Do not publish private data.
