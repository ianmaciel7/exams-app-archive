## 2026 edition and recency comparison (2026-11-27)
Public publisher catalog and search excerpts, not full books or exhaustive sales ranking. Editorial recommendations by research scope:
- [Designing Interfaces, 3rd Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-interfaces-3rd/9781492051954/) — January 2020; general interface pattern reference; no fourth edition located in this search. [Edition record](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-interfaces-3rd/9781492051954/copyright-page01.html).
- [Designing AI Interfaces](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-ai-interfaces/9798341639812/) — Louise Macfadyen, March 2026; human control, agent interaction, progress, planning, evaluation, input/output. [Portuguese translation](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/projetando-interfaces-de/0642572353162/).
- [UX Skills for Business Strategy](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ux-skills-for/9781098177867/) — March 2026; linking UX practices to business outcomes.
- [Articulating Design Decisions, 3rd Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/articulating-design-decisions/0642572326074/) — catalog date November 2026; stakeholder communication and decision rationale; some public preview pages still carry draft notices; verify final-edition availability.
- [AI in UX Design](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-in-ux/9781807787592/) — July 2026 catalog date; AI-assisted design workflows. Platform catalog includes other publishers; do not assume all are published by O'Reilly Media.
- [Designing and Prototyping Interfaces with Figma, 3rd Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-and-prototyping/9781805805892/) — January 2026; tool-specific practice, adaptive layouts; available in platform catalog.
- [AI for UX](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-for-ux/0642572224561/) and [Designing for AI](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-for-ai/0642572278793/) — December 2026 catalog dates, future as of review; drafts/advance entries, not verified as final published editions.
Priority: general pattern vocabulary + AI-native interaction patterns, then methods and stakeholder communication. Recency is not equivalent to relevance or quality. No claim of full-book reading, full publisher catalog coverage, or plugin release.
