## Cross-indexed reusable practices

- **semantic-token-references**: Use named roles and references instead of duplicated literals; validate token resolution and contrast.
- **surface-ladder**: Use ordered surfaces and optional hairline edges instead of indiscriminate shadows; verify separation in each theme and high contrast.
- **type-hierarchy**: Assign typography roles with family, size, weight, line height and tracking; validate fallbacks, zoom and reflow.
- **component-variants**: Specify purpose, geometry, token references, variants and states; test keyboard, touch, focus, disabled and error independently.
- **layout-responsive**: Define grid, density, breakpoints and collapse strategy; test actual narrow viewports rather than infer from desktop.
- **visual-restraint**: Use intentional accent roles, imagery and depth appropriate to content; avoid applying marketing hero defaults to application workspaces.
- **agent-ready-doc**: Use compact structured tokens plus prose rules and linked component contracts; verify schema and implementation, not merely visual resemblance.
- **evidence-boundaries**: Separate observed, inferred, recommended and missing; record source, date, scope and known gaps; do not claim product behavior from marketing screenshots.

## Gaps and recommendations

information architecture; application shell and real product flows; interaction states and keyboard navigation; motion timing and reduced motion; accessibility validation; component anatomy; data visualization; loading/empty/error states; themes; governance.

Keep DESIGN.md compact: identity, semantic tokens, layout, rules and references. Link authoritative IA, shell, components, interaction contracts, motion, accessibility, data visualization, content, themes and validation documents as needed. Do not confuse recommendations with required schema.
