## Verified design findings
The official design write-up explains a family of simple shapes and expressive eyes, with variants for identity. It documents idle, thinking, working, waiting, blocked and done. The avatar provides quick state feedback; hover reveals the action. The computer has graduated access (status, pinned preview, full takeover). Chat can combine text, interactive cards and events. Source: official design case study, sections “What are they doing”, “How much do I need to know”, “Their computer, not yours”, and “The shape of information”.

The designer's separate guide presents notch, corner and cursor-following experiments, plus adjustable springs, timing and easing using production assets. Those explorations are not evidence that all concepts shipped.
