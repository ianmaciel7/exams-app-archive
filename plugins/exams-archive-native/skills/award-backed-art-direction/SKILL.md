# Award-backed art direction and visual benchmarking

Read `references/award-backed-art-direction-2025-2026.md` (relative to this skill directory) before selecting a visual direction. This is the 2026-11-27 research snapshot; the canonical living record is https://github.com/ianmaciel7/exams-app-archive/blob/main/docs/references/ui-ux-research-sources/award-backed-art-direction-2025-2026.md and its adjacent structured JSON and source register. Recheck current award records and case studies when freshness matters.

- Do not default to the same familiar apps. Search the nine awards and relevant design studios, including recent years, then choose references by task fit, not prestige. Distinguish juried winner, audience winner, finalist and honoree; award date is not launch date. Separate product UI, editorial design, expressive brand site and marketing landing page.
- Establish the brief and actual task; compare distinct visual worlds, not just hue swaps. Evaluate typography, measure, hierarchy, density, iconography, surfaces, wordmark, role-based colors, feedback and responsive composition together. Avoid generic AI-template defaults where they compete with the task, not as universal prohibitions.
- Prefer authentic award/studio evidence and current product captures with provenance; record actual observation versus inference. Do not copy restricted assets, guess CSS/tokens, or infer interactions from images. Awards do not establish accessibility, efficacy or universal modernity.
- Test matched representative content and states, both themes, contrast and non-color cues, keyboard, mobile, readability and task success. Record uncertainty, rejected directions and owner approval; preserve product scope and fidelity gates. No automatic palette approval.
- Use shared UX knowledge and existing brand/color governance references; add verified discoveries to the canonical catalog with dates, links and limits, not duplicate independent research. For Exams App, respect the current stage and check dev before/after any project changes.
