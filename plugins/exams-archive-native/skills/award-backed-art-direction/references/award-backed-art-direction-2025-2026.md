## Reusable evaluation protocol

- Start from task, audience, positioning and reading/operation context, not a favorite hue; separate brand, editorial and daily-use interfaces.
- Compare distinct art directions together with typography, layout rhythm, surface hierarchy, iconography, wordmark and semantic color using identical representative tasks, states and content in both themes. Editorial and instrumental precision are hypotheses, not standards.
- Avoid generic template recolors, indiscriminate accent fills, unnecessary cards, ornamental gradients, excessive radii and decorative iconography when they compete with the task; these are not universal bans.
- Evaluate legibility, scanability, task hierarchy, semantic recognition, contrast, focus, non-color cues, state coverage, layout stability, responsive rendering and distinctiveness. Require real captures with provenance; do not infer CSS or interaction from still images.
- Preserve product scope and stage gates. Awards are not proof of usability or accessibility. Record accepted/rejected alternatives and uncertainty.

## Exploratory direction concepts for learning and knowledge products

These are original hypotheses, not awarded designs, research results, or approved product tokens. They derive from the product concepts of knowledge, mastery, discovery, connection, evolution and precision, without reusing previous project colors.

| Concept | Design rationale | Exploratory swatches (not validated tokens) |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge | Intellectual/editorial depth, contemporary scientific publications, clear typography and reading hierarchy | #F4F0E8, #DAD4C7, #22221F, #B76C32, #D9AD56 |
| Mastery | Professional preparation, instrumental precision, rigor, competence and progress | #F7F7F5, #D9DCD9, #262C30, #536D80, #CFB56C |
| Connections | Organization, curiosity and relationships among knowledge objects | #F5F4F1, #DDDAD6, #29242D, #885C77, #C3A0B7 |

Proposed qualitative task fit (hypothesis, not measured): Knowledge: high for questions, notes, graph and review; medium for mock exams. Mastery: high for questions, mock exams and review; medium for notes and graph. Connections: high for notes, graph and review; medium for questions and mock exams. Preferred exploration: Knowledge + Mastery, editorial reading quality with professional software precision. Do not treat the preference or swatches as approved identity. Compare complete matched workspace studies in both themes, with actual question content and feedback, accessibility checks and owner review. Future features are context for scalable identity, not authorization to expand the current release. Keep the grayscale High-Fidelity stage intact until its separate review gate.

Brand brief hypothesis: a serious learning and knowledge workspace supporting preparation, practice, assessment, understanding gaps, organization and review. Intended qualities: intellectually engaging, mature, precise, organized and approachable; avoid a generic SaaS dashboard, childish gamification or bureaucratic aesthetic.
